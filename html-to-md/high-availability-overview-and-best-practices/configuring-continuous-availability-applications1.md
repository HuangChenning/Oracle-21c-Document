## 40 Configuring Continuous Availability for Applications

Depending on your application's high availability requirements, you can implement the level of high availability (HA) protection that you need.

- [Configuring Level 1: Basic Application High Availability](configuring-level-1-basic-application-high-availability.md)
- [Configuring Level 2: Prepare Applications for Planned Maintenance](configuring-level-2-prepare-applications-planned-maintenance.md)
- [Configuring Level 3: Mask Unplanned and Planned Failovers from Applications](configuring-level-3-mask-unplanned-and-planned-failovers-applications.md)

The primary audience for this document is application developers and application owners. Operational examples are included for database administrators and PDB administrators.

In [About Application High Availability Levels](application-high-availability-levels.md), the three HA protection levels and terms are defined, and software recommendations are listed.
