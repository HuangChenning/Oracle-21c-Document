## 7.706 USER\_SUBSCR\_REGISTRATIONS

`USER_SUBSCR_REGISTRATIONS` displays information about the subscription registrations owned by the current user. Its columns are the same as those in `DBA_SUBSCR_REGISTRATIONS`.
