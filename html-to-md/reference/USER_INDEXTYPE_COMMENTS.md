## 7.524 USER\_INDEXTYPE\_COMMENTS

`USER_INDEXTYPE_COMMENTS` displays comments for the user-defined indextypes owned by the current user. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_INDEXTYPE_COMMENTS`.
