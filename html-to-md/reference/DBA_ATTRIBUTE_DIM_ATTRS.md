## 5.142 DBA\_ATTRIBUTE\_DIM\_ATTRS

`DBA_ATTRIBUTE_DIM_ATTRS` describes the attributes of all attribute dimensions in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_ATTRIBUTE_DIM_ATTRS`.
