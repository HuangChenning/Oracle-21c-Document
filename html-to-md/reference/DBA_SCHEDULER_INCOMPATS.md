## 6.328 DBA\_SCHEDULER\_INCOMPATS

`DBA_SCHEDULER_INCOMPATS` displays all Scheduler incompatibility resource objects in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_SCHEDULER_INCOMPATS`.
