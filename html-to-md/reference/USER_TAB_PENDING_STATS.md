## 7.719 USER\_TAB\_PENDING\_STATS

`USER_TAB_PENDING_STATS` describes pending statistics for tables, partitions, and subpartitions owned by the current user. Its columns (except for `OWNER`) are the same as those in `ALL_TAB_PENDING_STATS`.
