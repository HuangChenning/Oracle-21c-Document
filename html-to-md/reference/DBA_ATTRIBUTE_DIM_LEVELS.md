## 5.152 DBA\_ATTRIBUTE\_DIM\_LEVELS

`DBA_ATTRIBUTE_DIM_LEVELS` describes the levels of all attribute dimensions in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_ATTRIBUTE_DIM_LEVELS`.
