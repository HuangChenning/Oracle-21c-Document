## 6.316 DBA\_SCHEDULER\_CHAIN\_RULES

`DBA_SCHEDULER_CHAIN_RULES` displays information about the rules for all chains in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_SCHEDULER_CHAIN_RULES`.
