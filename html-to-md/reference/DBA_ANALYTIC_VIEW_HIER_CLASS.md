## 5.86 DBA\_ANALYTIC\_VIEW\_HIER\_CLASS

`DBA_ANALYTIC_VIEW_HIER_CLASS` describes the classifications of the hierarchies in all analytic views in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_ANALYTIC_VIEW_HIER_CLASS`.
