## 6.317 DBA\_SCHEDULER\_CHAIN\_STEPS

`DBA_SCHEDULER_CHAIN_STEPS` displays information about the defined steps of all chains in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_SCHEDULER_CHAIN_STEPS`.
