## 5.359 DBA\_HIER\_HIER\_ATTR\_CLASS

`DBA_HIER_HIER_ATTR_CLASS` describes the classifications of the hierarchical attributes of all hierarchies in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_HIER_HIER_ATTR_CLASS`.
