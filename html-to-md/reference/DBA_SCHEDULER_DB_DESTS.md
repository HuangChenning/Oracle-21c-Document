## 6.320 DBA\_SCHEDULER\_DB\_DESTS

`DBA_SCHEDULER_DB_DESTS` displays information about all destination objects in the database pointing to remote databases. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_SCHEDULER_DB_DESTS`.
