## 6.204 DBA\_NESTED\_TABLE\_COLS

`DBA_NESTED_TABLE_COLS` describes the columns of all nested tables in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_NESTED_TABLE_COLS`.

To gather statistics for this view, use the `DBMS_STATS` package.
