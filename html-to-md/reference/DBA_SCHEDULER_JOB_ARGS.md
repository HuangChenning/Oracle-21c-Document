## 6.329 DBA\_SCHEDULER\_JOB\_ARGS

`DBA_SCHEDULER_JOB_ARGS` displays information about the arguments of all Scheduler jobs in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_SCHEDULER_JOB_ARGS`.
