## 6.330 DBA\_SCHEDULER\_JOB\_CLASSES

`DBA_SCHEDULER_JOB_CLASSES` displays information about all Scheduler job classes in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_SCHEDULER_JOB_CLASSES`.
