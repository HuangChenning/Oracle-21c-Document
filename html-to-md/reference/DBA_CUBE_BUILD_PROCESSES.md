## 5.254 DBA\_CUBE\_BUILD\_PROCESSES

`DBA_CUBE_BUILD_PROCESSES` describes all OLAP build processes and maintenance scripts in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_CUBE_BUILD_PROCESSES`.
