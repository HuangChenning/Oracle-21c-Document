## 7.142 DBA\_XML\_SCHEMA\_SUBSTGRP\_MBRS

`DBA_XML_SCHEMA_SUBSTGRP_MBRS` describes all members of substitution groups. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_XML_SCHEMA_SUBSTGRP_MBRS`.
