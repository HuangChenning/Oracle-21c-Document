## 7.245 SESSION\_ROLES

`SESSION_ROLES` describes the roles that are currently enabled to the user.

| Column | Datatype | NULL | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ROLE` | `VARCHAR2(128)` | `NOT NULL` | Name of the role |
