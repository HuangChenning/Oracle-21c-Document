## 7.768 USER\_XTERNAL\_PART\_TABLES

`USER_XTERNAL_PART_TABLES` describes object-level information for partitioned external tables owned by the current user. Its columns (except for `OWNER`) are the same as those in `ALL_XTERNAL_PART_TABLES`.
