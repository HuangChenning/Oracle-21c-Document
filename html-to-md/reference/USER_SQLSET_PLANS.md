## 7.683 USER\_SQLSET\_PLANS

`USER_SQLSET_PLANS` describes captured plans for statements in the SQL tuning sets owned by the current user. Its columns (except for `SQLSET_OWNER`) are the same as those in `ALL_SQLSET_PLANS`.
