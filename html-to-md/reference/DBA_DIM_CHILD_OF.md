## 5.285 DBA\_DIM\_CHILD\_OF

`DBA_DIM_CHILD_OF` describes a hierarchical relationship of 1 to n between all the pairs of levels in the dimensions in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_DIM_CHILD_OF`.
