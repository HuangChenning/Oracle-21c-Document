## 7.335 USER\_ANALYTIC\_VIEW\_LEVEL\_CLASS

`USER_ANALYTIC_VIEW_LEVEL_CLASS` describes the level classifications of the analytic views owned by the current user. Its columns (except for `OWNER`) are the same as those in `ALL_ANALYTIC_VIEW_LEVEL_CLASS`.
