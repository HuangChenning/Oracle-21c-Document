## 5.116 DBA\_APPLY\_DML\_CONF\_HANDLERS

`DBA_APPLY_DML_CONF_HANDLERS` provides details about DML conflict handlers. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_APPLY_DML_CONF_HANDLERS`.
