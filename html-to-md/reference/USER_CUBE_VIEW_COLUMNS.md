## 7.435 USER\_CUBE\_VIEW\_COLUMNS

`USER_CUBE_VIEW_COLUMNS` describes the columns of relational views of OLAP cubes owned by the current user. Its columns (except for `OWNER`) are the same as those in `ALL_CUBE_VIEW_COLUMNS`.
