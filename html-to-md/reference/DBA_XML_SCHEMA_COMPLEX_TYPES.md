## 7.137 DBA\_XML\_SCHEMA\_COMPLEX\_TYPES

`DBA_XML_SCHEMA_COMPLEX_TYPES` describes all complex types in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_XML_SCHEMA_COMPLEX_TYPES`.
