## 7.314 USER\_ANALYTIC\_VIEW\_COLUMNS

`USER_ANALYTIC_VIEW_COLUMNS` describes the columns of the analytic views owned by the current user. Its columns (except for `OWNER`) are the same as those in `ALL_ANALYTIC_VIEW_COLUMNS`.
