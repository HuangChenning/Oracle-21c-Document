## 7.422 USER\_CUBE\_DIM\_LEVELS

`USER_CUBE_DIM_LEVELS` describes the OLAP dimension levels owned by the current user. Its columns (except for `OWNER`) are the same as those in `ALL_CUBE_DIM_LEVELS`.
