## 7.279 USER\_ADVISOR\_OBJECTS

`USER_ADVISOR_OBJECTS` displays information about the objects currently referenced by the advisors owned by the current user. Its columns (except for `OWNER`, `ATTR21`, and `ATTR22`) are the same as those in `DBA_ADVISOR_OBJECTS`.
