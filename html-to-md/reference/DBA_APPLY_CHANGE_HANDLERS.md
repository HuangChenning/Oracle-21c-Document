## 5.114 DBA\_APPLY\_CHANGE\_HANDLERS

`DBA_APPLY_CHANGE_HANDLERS` displays information about the change handlers on all tables in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_APPLY_CHANGE_HANDLERS`.
