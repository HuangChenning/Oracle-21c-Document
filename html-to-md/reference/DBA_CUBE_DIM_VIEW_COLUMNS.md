## 5.258 DBA\_CUBE\_DIM\_VIEW\_COLUMNS

`DBA_CUBE_DIM_VIEW_COLUMNS` describes the columns of the relational views of all OLAP cube dimensions in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_CUBE_DIM_VIEW_COLUMNS`.
