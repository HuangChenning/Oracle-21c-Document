## 5.156 DBA\_ATTRIBUTE\_DIM\_LVL\_CLASS

`DBA_ATTRIBUTE_DIM_LVL_CLASS` describes the level classifications of all attribute dimensions in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_ATTRIBUTE_DIM_LVL_CLASS`.
