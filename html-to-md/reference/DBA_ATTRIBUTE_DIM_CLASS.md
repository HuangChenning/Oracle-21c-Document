## 5.144 DBA\_ATTRIBUTE\_DIM\_CLASS

`DBA_ATTRIBUTE_DIM_CLASS` describes the classifications of all attribute dimensions in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_ATTRIBUTE_DIM_CLASS`.
