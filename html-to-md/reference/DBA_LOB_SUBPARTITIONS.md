## 6.147 DBA\_LOB\_SUBPARTITIONS

`DBA_LOB_SUBPARTITIONS` displays partition-level attributes of all LOB data subpartitions in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_LOB_SUBPARTITIONS`.
