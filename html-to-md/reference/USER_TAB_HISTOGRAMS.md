## 7.715 USER\_TAB\_HISTOGRAMS

`USER_TAB_HISTOGRAMS` describes histograms on columns of tables owned by the current user. Its columns (except for `OWNER`) are the same as those in `ALL_TAB_HISTOGRAMS`.
