## 7.438 USER\_DATAPUMP\_JOBS

`USER_DATAPUMP_JOBS` displays the Data Pump jobs owned by the current user. Its columns (except for `OWNER_NAME`) are the same as those in `DBA_DATAPUMP_JOBS`.
