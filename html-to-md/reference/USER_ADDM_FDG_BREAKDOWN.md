## 7.266 USER\_ADDM\_FDG\_BREAKDOWN

`USER_ADDM_FDG_BREAKDOWN` describes the contribution for each finding from the different instances owned by the current user. Its columns are the same as those in `DBA_ADDM_FDG_BREAKDOWN`.
