## 6.109 DBA\_IND\_PENDING\_STATS

`DBA_IND_PENDING_STATS` describes pending statistics for all tables, partitions, and subpartitions in the database collected using the `DBMS_STATS` package. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_IND_PENDING_STATS`.
