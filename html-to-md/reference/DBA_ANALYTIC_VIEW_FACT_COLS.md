## 5.83 DBA\_ANALYTIC\_VIEW\_FACT\_COLS

`DBA_ANALYTIC_VIEW_FACT_COLS` describes the fact columns of all analytic views in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_ANALYTIC_VIEW_FACT_COLS`.

Note:

This view is available starting with Oracle Database 21c.
