## 7.165 DBA\_XTERNAL\_LOC\_SUBPARTITIONS

`DBA_XTERNAL_LOC_SUBPARTITIONS` describes subpartition-level locations in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_XTERNAL_LOC_SUBPARTITIONS`.
