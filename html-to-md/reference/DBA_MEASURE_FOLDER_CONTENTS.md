## 6.173 DBA\_MEASURE\_FOLDER\_CONTENTS

`DBA_MEASURE_FOLDER_CONTENTS` describes the contents of all OLAP measure folders in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_MEASURE_FOLDER_CONTENTS`.
