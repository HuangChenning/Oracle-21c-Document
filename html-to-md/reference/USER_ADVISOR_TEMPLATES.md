## 7.296 USER\_ADVISOR\_TEMPLATES

`USER_ADVISOR_TEMPLATES` displays information about the templates owned by the current user. Its columns (except for `OWNER`) are the same as those in `DBA_ADVISOR_TEMPLATES`.
