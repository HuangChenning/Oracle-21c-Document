## 5.166 DBA\_ATTRIBUTE\_TRANSFORMATIONS

`DBA_ATTRIBUTE_TRANSFORMATIONS` describes the transformation functions for all transformations in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_ATTRIBUTE_TRANSFORMATIONS`.
