## 7.525 USER\_INDEXTYPE\_OPERATORS

`USER_INDEXTYPE_OPERATORS` lists all the operators supported by indextypes owned by the current user. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_INDEXTYPE_OPERATORS`.
