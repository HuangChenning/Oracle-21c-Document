## 5.57 DBA\_ANALYTIC\_VIEW\_AGGR\_FNS

`DBA_ANALYTIC_VIEW_AGGR_FNS` describes the aggregation functions of all analytic views in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_ANALYTIC_VIEW_AGGR_FNS`.

Note:

This view is available starting with Oracle Database 21c.
