## 7.5 DBA\_STREAMS\_MESSAGE\_CONSUMERS

`DBA_STREAMS_MESSAGE_CONSUMERS` displays information about all Streams messaging clients in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_STREAMS_MESSAGE_CONSUMERS`.
