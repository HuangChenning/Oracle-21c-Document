## 5.268 DBA\_CUBE\_SUB\_PARTITION\_LEVELS

`DBA_CUBE_SUB_PARTITION_LEVELS` describes the OLAP secondary partition levels in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_CUBE_SUB_PARTITION_LEVELS`.
