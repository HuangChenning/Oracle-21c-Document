## 6.245 DBA\_PLSQL\_TYPE\_ATTRS

`DBA_PLSQL_TYPE_ATTRS` describes the attributes of all PL/SQL types in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_PLSQL_TYPE_ATTRS`.
