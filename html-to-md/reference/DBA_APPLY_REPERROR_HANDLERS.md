## 5.130 DBA\_APPLY\_REPERROR\_HANDLERS

`DBA_APPLY_REPERROR_HANDLERS` provides details about apply reperror handlers. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_APPLY_REPERROR_HANDLERS`.
