## 5.226 DBA\_CLUSTERING\_JOINS

`DBA_CLUSTERING_JOINS` describes joins to the dimension tables associated with all tables with an attribute clustering clause in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_CLUSTERING_JOINS`.
