## 7.625 USER\_QUEUE\_SCHEDULES

`USER_QUEUE_SCHEDULES` describes the propagation schedules whose source queues are owned by the current user. Its columns (except for `SCHEMA`) are the same as those in `ALL_QUEUE_SCHEDULES`.
