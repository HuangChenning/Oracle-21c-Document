## 5.66 DBA\_ANALYTIC\_VIEW\_BASE\_MEAS

`DBA_ANALYTIC_VIEW_BASE_MEAS` describes the base measures in all analytic views in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_ANALYTIC_VIEW_BASE_MEAS`.
