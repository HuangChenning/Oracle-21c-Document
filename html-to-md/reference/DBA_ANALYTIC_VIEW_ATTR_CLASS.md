## 5.61 DBA\_ANALYTIC\_VIEW\_ATTR\_CLASS

`DBA_ANALYTIC_VIEW_ATTR_CLASS` describes the attribute classifications of all analytic views in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_ANALYTIC_VIEW_ATTR_CLASS`.
