## 7.657 USER\_SCHEDULER\_JOB\_LOG

`USER_SCHEDULER_JOB_LOG` displays log information for the Scheduler jobs owned by the current user. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_SCHEDULER_JOB_LOG`.
